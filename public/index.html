Google Play Store badge generator

Make your Play Store stats visible. Generate a live badge for any Android app, then copy a URL or ready-to-paste Markdown for your README.

Badges for your Android app

App card

A card with the app's icon, title, developer, downloads, and rating. It has light and dark themes and can follow the reader's system theme on GitHub.

/badge/full?id=<package>&theme=light|dark

Downloads badge

Shows the app's install count from Google Play, either exact or compacted (for example 10K).

/badge/downloads?id=<package>[&pretty]

Rating badge

Shows the average Play Store rating and the number of ratings, or a row of stars.

/badge/ratings?id=<package>[&pretty]

Version badge

Shows the latest published version, with fallback text for apps whose version varies by device.

/badge/version?id=<package>[&fallback=Varies]

Where do I find the package ID?

Open the app on play.google.com. The package ID is the part after id= in the URL, for example com.example.app.

Do the badges stay up to date?

Yes. Each badge is rendered on demand from the current Play Store listing. All badges also accept country, and the small badges accept label.
